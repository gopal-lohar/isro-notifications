November 13, 2026

Indian delegation led by Dr. Jayaprakash V. Thomas, Director, EDOP, ISRO Headquarters participated in the Fifth Ministerial Conference on Space Applications for Sustainable Development in Asia and the Pacific (MC-5) Organised by United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (UN ESCAP) at United Nations Conference Centre (UNCC), Bangkok, Thailand on 28th & 29th October 2026.

Chairman, ISRO/Secretary, DoS addressed the conference, in the opening sessions, through a Video message along with the opening address by Ms. Armida Salsiah Alisjahbana, Executive Secretary of ESCAP and Special opening address by H.E. Mr. Yodchanan Wongsawat, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Higher Education, Science, Research, and Innovation of Thailand. In his video message Chairman, ISRO/Secretary, DoS highlighted the long and successful association of ISRO with UN-ESCAP and the achievements made by Indian space fraternity in space applications for sustainable development. Chairman, ISRO/Secretary, DoS also invited ESCAP member countries to engage with Indian Space Sector Startups and Academia to advance the space sector applications for achieving sustainable development in their respective regions.

Dr. Jayaprakash V. Thomas, Director, EDPO, ISRO Hq. presented the country statement on agenda ‘From Data to Action - Harnessing Space Applications for a Resilient and Inclusive Future through Enhanced Regional Cooperation’ and also apprised the conference on the ‘Progress made by India in the implementation of the Jakarta Ministerial Declaration and the Asia-Pacific Plan of Action on Space Applications for Sustainable Development (2018–2030), Phase II (2022–2026)’.

Photo 3: Indian Delegation at the Conference

On the second day of MC-5 (29th October 2029) Dr. Jayaprakash V. Thomas, Director, EDOP participated, as invited panellist, in a High-Level Regional Dialogue ‘Sagarmatha to the Sea: Lessons from Nepal's Bhotekoshi-Trishuli Disaster for Strengthening Resilience to Cascading Hazards in Asia and the Pacific’. He highlighted the capabilities of Earth Observation missions in detecting and monitoring the precursors of cascading natural hazards in hilly terrain. He further emphasised on need for seamless integration of Space and Ground observations, on continues bases, for operational early detection and forewarning for natural disasters in the Himalayan region.

Photo 4: Dr. Jayaprakash V. Thomas, Director, EDPO as panellist in a High-Level Regional Dialogue ‘Sagarmatha to the Sea’ during the second day of MC-5

The conference also provided a platform for ISRO delegation to interact with international delegates from other space agencies, government ministries of members countries and UN offices. H.E. Mr. Biraj Bhakta Shrestha, Minister of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation of Nepal while appreciating support by Indian Government also expressed keen interest in strengthening the co-operation, between Nepal and India, in sectors of space applications for disaster early warning in the hilly area and capacity building.

Photo 5: Members of ISRO/Indian delegation in discussion with ESA delegation.

The conference concluded with adoption of the ministerial declaration on space applications for sustainable development in Asia and the Pacific, 2026 (Bangkok Declaration-2026).

Preceding to MC-5, ESCAP organised Thirtieth Session of the Intergovernmental Consultative Committee (ICC-30) on the Regional Space Applications Programme for Sustainable Development in Asia and the Pacific (RESAP) on 26th and 27th October. Two officials from ISRO, Shri. Rajiv Kumar, Group Director, SR & LUMG, RSAA, NRSC and Dr. Bhaskar R. Nikam, DD, EDPO participated in ICC-30 and presented the country reports on ‘Progress in implementing the Asia-Pacific Plan of Action on Space Applications for Sustainable Development (2018–2030)’ and ‘Implementation of the Jakarta Ministerial Declaration on Space Applications for Sustainable Development in Asia and the Pacific’. Additional delegates from NRSC/ISRO virtually participated in the side event of ICC-30 on ‘Expert Group Meeting: Harnessing Earth Observation for Agricultural Drought Resilience: Sustainable Drought Monitoring Systems in Central Asia’. Dr. V. M. Chowdary, Group Director, AS&AG, RSAA, NRSC presented India’s experience in leveraging earth observations in assessment to early warning of Agricultural drought, in this session.
